Fuel

Food is energy

Simple ideas to power your dancing — and keep it fun.
💛 This bit is all about fuelling your dancing — energy, strong bones and happy recovery. It's never about eating less. If anything ever feels tricky with food or how you feel, chat to your mum, dad or coach.

Before you dance

🍌
Banana + peanut butter
Quick energy that lasts. 30–60 min before class.
🥯
Toast or a bagel + honey
Easy fuel that won't sit heavy.
🍎
Apple + a handful of nuts
Grab-and-go for the studio bag.

After you dance

🥛
Milk or a yoghurt smoothie
Protein + calcium to rebuild and grow strong bones.
🍗
Chicken or beans + rice
A proper refuel after a big session.
🧀
Cheese & crackers
Fast, tasty recovery when dinner's a while off.

Studio-bag snacks

🥨 Pretzels🫐 Berries 🍓 Fruit pot🥜 Trail mix 🧃 Water + squash🥕 Carrot sticks 🍪 Oat cookie
Growing dancers need lots of fuel. Eat the rainbow, drink your water. 🌈
